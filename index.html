01 / About

Engineering Beyond the Interface.

I’m a full-stack engineer who has worked on mobile apps, web platforms and backend services. Day to day that means Flutter on the phone, Angular in the browser and Java with Spring Boot behind both.

What I enjoy most is following a system end to end: shaping the domain model, agreeing an API contract, writing the business rules, locking down the endpoints, wiring in other services, and then seeing it land in a screen someone actually uses.

Much of my recent work sits in enterprise merchant platforms: ERP-style workflows, restaurant management, and a plugin architecture where independently built business modules plug into one merchant app. I care about clear boundaries, security, tests, and choosing the practical option over the clever one.

I’m steadily deepening my knowledge of distributed systems, cloud, system design, Python and AI engineering.
